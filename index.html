01

About Me 🤣

I am proud to be a young man with principles. People describe me in different ways, and honestly, I sometimes find their descriptions funny.

Some people call me "mkauzu". I accept it. The funny thing is that I never planned to become that kind of person. Somehow, I just discovered myself that way.

02

The Listener & The Funny One 😁

Some people see me as someone who listens carefully. Others know me as the funny one — the person who jokes and makes people laugh.

Even I don't always understand where that side of me comes from.

When I am not around, some of those people actually miss me and start teasing me about it when I return.

03

Maybe I Am A Little Crazy 🤣

Some people probably think I am confused or crazy.

Sometimes I laugh for absolutely no obvious reason. And honestly... I admit it.

Maybe I am not completely okay. Maybe I am just a little crazy. 😂

04

Don't Judge Me Too Quickly

Some people think I see myself as better than everyone else. But that is not true.

Sometimes people see confidence and mistake it for pride. Sometimes jealousy can also change the way someone sees you.

I can enjoy being around people and friends, but trusting people is not easy for me. I don't like becoming too familiar with everyone.

I believe I am trustworthy and faithful. But the real question is... How do you see me?

05

I Don't Need Many Friends

I don't need a huge number of friends.

When someone comes with unnecessary "superstar" behavior, I simply distance myself.

I don't like nonsense, especially when I am focused on something important.

06
